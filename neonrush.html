Privacy Policy for Neon Rush

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Developer: HexSoft

App: Neon Rush (Android package com.hexsoft.rungrush)

Contact: hexsoft.support@gmail.com

The short version

Neon Rush does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information.
The game works entirely on your device and has no internet access.

What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress, the game stores a small amount of data locally on your device (in the app's private storage). It never leaves your device. It consists of:

  • your coins, best distance and best sector reached
  • which vehicles you own, which vehicle and which road you have selected
  • missions, trophies, pilot rank and experience, daily challenge and daily gift streaks
  • your settings (sound effects, music and vibration on or off) and whether you have seen the tutorial

This data contains no name, email address, account, location, device identifier or any other information that identifies you. The app does not use Android's automatic cloud backup, so the app does not copy this data anywhere. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Neon Rush > Storage, or by uninstalling the game.

Information the game does not collect

Neon Rush does not collect or use:

  • personal information of any kind (name, email, phone number, address, date of birth)
  • location, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or any other sensor data
  • advertising IDs or any other device or app identifiers
  • usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics sent by the game itself
  • payment information (the game has no purchases for real money; vehicles are bought with coins earned by playing)

Permissions

The game requests a single Android permission:

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): used only to give short haptic feedback during play. You can switch vibration off in the game's settings.

The game does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot connect to a network.

Advertising and third parties

Neon Rush contains no advertising and no third-party SDKs, analytics or tracking tools. No data is shared with or sold to any third party.

Google Play

If you install the game from Google Play, Google may provide developers with aggregated, anonymous technical statistics about an app (for example crash and "app not responding" rates) under Google's own terms and your device settings. That is handled by Google, not by the game, and it contains no personal information about you. See the Google Play privacy information.

Children

Neon Rush is intended for players aged 13 and over. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example by adding a feature that uses the internet), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
