Privacy Policy — Bee Player

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Developer: HexSoft

Application Package: com.hexsoft.beeplayer

1. Overview

Bee Player is a 100% offline video and music player developed by HexSoft. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Bee Player does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or user media.

2. Data Collection & Offline Operation

  • Zero Personal Data Collected: Bee Player does not require account registration, login, email addresses, phone numbers, or personal identifiers.
  • Zero Network Transmission: Bee Player does not include advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, crash-reporting servers, or remote telemetry. All video playback, music playback, playlists, search history, watch progress, and private vault files remain strictly on your local Android device.

3. Permissions Used & Why

Bee Player requests only the minimum Android permissions required for core offline media playback:

  • Videos & Audio (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and below): Used solely to scan and play video and audio files stored locally on your device and SD card.
  • Media Playback Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Allows uninterrupted background audio playback and system media controls when the screen is off or you switch apps.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Displays playback controls in the notification shade and optional local alerts when new offline media files are added to your device.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Prevents playback interruption during continuous audio/video playback.

4. Local Storage & Private Folder

  • Playlists, watch resume positions, preferences, and PIN-locked Private Folder videos are stored exclusively inside the app's private local storage directory on your device.
  • You can clear your search history, watch history, thumbnail cache, or uninstall the app at any time to permanently remove all locally stored app data.

5. Children's Privacy

Bee Player does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in a future release, the updated policy will be published with a new effective date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact HexSoft via the developer email listed on the Bee Player Google Play Store page.
