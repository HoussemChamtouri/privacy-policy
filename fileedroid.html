Privacy Policy — fileedroid

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 · Developer: Hexsoft · Package: com.hexsoft.fileedroid · Contact: hexsoft.support@gmail.com

1. Overview

fileedroid is a 100% offline file converter and visual whiteboard application built by Hexsoft. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data or user files.

2. Data Collection & Offline Processing

  • No Personal Data Collected: fileedroid requires no account, login, email address, or personal identifier.
  • 100% On-Device Processing: All file previews, resizing, filtering, archiving, and conversions execute strictly locally on your device. No files are ever uploaded to external servers.
  • No Ads, Analytics, or Tracking: fileedroid contains no advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or behavioral trackers. The Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) permission is explicitly removed from the app manifest.

3. Permissions & Local Storage

  • Android System Pickers (SAF & MediaStore): fileedroid accesses only the files you explicitly pick or share into the app, and saves outputs only to your chosen folder or Downloads directory without requiring broad storage permissions.
  • Private On-Device Workspace: Whiteboard state, zoom settings, and preferences are saved locally in private app storage. You can delete individual files, clear any page, clear temporary cache in Settings, or uninstall the app at any time to permanently erase all local data.

4. Children's Privacy

Because fileedroid operates entirely offline and collects zero personal data from any user, it complies with COPPA and GDPR-K requirements.
